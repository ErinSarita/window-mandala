# The Window Mandala

A map of the window of capacity, with the Self at its center: the four directions a nervous system moves under threat, the movements between them, and the way home, with practices for widening the window.

A companion to [The Return Mandala](https://erinsarita.github.io/return-mandala/) and [The Pregnancy and Birth Mandala](https://erinsarita.github.io/prenatal-mandala/). Created by Erin Singleton, a student in APPPAH's Prenatal & Perinatal Educator certification. A model for reflection and education, not diagnosis, therapy, or medical advice.

## The model

A synthesis of earlier models, described in our own words: the window of tolerance (Siegel), the ladder of states (Porges, Dana), the defense cascade (Schauer and Elbert), fawn (Walker), tend and befriend (Taylor and colleagues), the thaw and orienting (Levine), the Self and its parts (Schwartz, Internal Family Systems), and the science of resilience (Harvard's Center on the Developing Child). The circle, the four directions, the healthy root of each defense, and the transitions are this mandala's own synthesis, theory-based and offered from an evolutionary perspective.

- **The center:** the Self in two layers: the Flower of Life at its heart, drawn as a sun, and a ring of the eight qualities of Self around it, with rays and warmth spilling into the gold window.
- **Inside the window:** four healthy states, one per direction: energized, still, settled, connected. The dashed line is the window's edge, with four gold doorways for orienting.
- **At the edge:** the early signals: revving, bracing, sinking, over-tuning.
- **Outside the window:** fight or flight (top), freeze (right), collapse (bottom), fawn (left). The outer and edge rings flow in a gradient round the circle, each color melting into its neighbor's.
- **Around the outside:** arrows for the eight movements between neighboring defenses, both ways, and a braid weaving the whole nervous system: gold (safe and connected), red (fight or flight), purple (freeze), blue (collapse), pink (fawn).
- **Your window:** a slider that widens the Self and the window and thins the rings of threat. The "How wide is mine?" reflection sets it.

Tabs: How to read, Where am I? (a three-question finder and an eight-question window reflection), The Self, The four directions, Moving between, Patterns, Triggers and regulation, and Practices (breathing with the Self and rings of kindness both animate the wheel).

## The file

The mandala is the single file `index.html`. The content lives in constants near the top of the script: `DIRS` for the four directions and their three layers, `MOVES` and `CROSSINGS` for the transitions, `HOME`, `SELF`, `PATTERNS`, `REG`, `PRACTICES`, `WIDTH`, `FINDER`, and `MODEL_NOTE`. Nothing a visitor answers is saved; only the light or dark choice is kept, under the `wm-` key.
