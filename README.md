# The Window Mandala

A map of the window of capacity, with the Self at its center: the four directions a nervous system moves under threat, the movements between them, and the way home, with practices for widening the window.

A companion to [The Return Mandala](https://erinsarita.github.io/return-mandala/) and [The Pregnancy and Birth Mandala](https://erinsarita.github.io/prenatal-mandala/). Created by Erin Singleton, a student in APPPAH's Prenatal & Perinatal Educator certification. A model for reflection and education, not diagnosis, therapy, or medical advice.

## The model

A synthesis of earlier models, described in our own words: the window of tolerance (Siegel), the ladder of states (Porges, Dana), the defense cascade (Schauer and Elbert), fawn (Walker), tend and befriend (Taylor and colleagues), the thaw and orienting (Levine), the Self and its parts (Schwartz, Internal Family Systems), and the science of resilience (Harvard's Center on the Developing Child). The circle, the four directions, the healthy root of each defense, and the transitions are this mandala's own synthesis, theory-based and offered from an evolutionary perspective.

- **The center:** the Self as a lotus: the Flower of Life drawn in gold at its heart, its central circle (the one holding the six-petaled flower) emphasized in light, and one ring of eight large lotus petals around it, one for each quality of Self, reaching out over the inner edge of the window, with a soft yellow glow.
- **Inside the window:** one continuous soft-yellow ring that blends between four healthy states, one per direction: energized, still, settled, connected. Each is the regulated, safety-held form of the defense beyond it (Deb Dana's blended states). Its edge is marked by a soft band of light, with four gold doorways for orienting. The edge is a threshold that works both ways: its hover card and panel give the signs of leaving and of coming back in.
- **At the edge:** the early signals: revving, bracing, sinking, over-tuning.
- **Outside the window:** fight or flight (top), freeze (right), collapse (bottom), fawn (left), as one continuous ring: the colors flow into one another round the circle with no border between them.
- **The movements:** at each junction between two defenses, two slender tapered arrows, one in each direction (eight in all). The braid is a three-strand plait of the three nervous systems: gold for ventral (safe and connected), red for sympathetic (fight or flight), and blue for dorsal (collapse).
- **Look and feel:** a soft pastel palette on warm ivory (a deep plum night in dark mode, where the window stays yellow), Cormorant Garamond for the wheel and headings, and a halo of light round the ring. Hovering lights only the section under the pointer.
- **Your window:** a slider that widens the Self and the window and thins the rings of threat. The "How wide is mine?" reflection sets it.

Tabs: How to read, Where am I? (a three-question finder and an eight-question window reflection), The Self, The four directions, Moving between, Patterns, Triggers and regulation, and Practices (breathing with the Self and rings of kindness both animate the wheel).

## The file

The mandala is the single file `index.html`. The content lives in constants near the top of the script: `DIRS` for the four directions and their three layers, `MOVES` and `CROSSINGS` for the transitions, `HOME`, `SELF`, `PATTERNS`, `REG`, `PRACTICES`, `WIDTH`, `FINDER`, and `MODEL_NOTE`. Nothing a visitor answers is saved; only the light or dark choice is kept, under the `wm-` key.

## Guided practices

The two buttons under the wheel start guided sessions of about two minutes, shown in a card beneath the wheel, with gentle sound (on by default) and an optional spoken voice (the browser's own speech).

- **Breathe with the Self:** a short settling-in, then ten breaths of five seconds in and five out, with on-screen cues, a breath counter, soft rising and falling tones, and the Self swelling and softening in time.
- **Rings of kindness:** the loving-kindness phrases through five circles (yourself, someone you love, someone you barely know, someone difficult, all beings), with a bell at each circle and rings of light that reach further each time: the window, its edge, the defenses, the braid.
